The Car

Aquila
Synergy

Built by Aquila Racing Cars — Scandinavia's largest racing car manufacturer. An aluminium monocoque, fully adjustable suspension and an ultra-light 410 kg chassis: a real racing car that teaches you how to drive a racer.

The car that secured Kevin Magnussen's first Formula championship came from the same Danish workshop.

Tested in England, the Synergy produced the lowest running costs the test teams had seen in over 20 years — and won along the way.
