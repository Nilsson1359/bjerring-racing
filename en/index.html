Race control · Season finale · Ring Djursland

Decided on the final weekend

Mason needed 67 points to secure P3 in the Junior championship, on a track he had never driven, with just 10 minutes of practice. He still qualified P3.

QualifyingP3

Only 10 minutes of practice on a brand new track, and still P3 in qualifying.

Heat 1P5

Hit hard from behind while running P3 and pushed half off the track. Fought back up to the leaders.

Heat 2P8

Up to P2, but contact during an overtake brought a Stop & Go penalty and a drop to P10. Won back two places.

Heat 3P5

Needed a top 7 finish. Drove smart on the wet track, and P3 in the championship was secured. 🥉

"First season in Formula 1000: DONE! Next year we go for gold." 🚀
